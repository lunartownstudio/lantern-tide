Support

Questions, bug reports or feedback? Email lunartownstudio@gmail.com and include your iPhone model and iOS version if you're reporting a problem.

Is my progress saved?

Yes. Your run, levels, Blessings, Inn Tokens, renovations and settings save automatically on your device whenever a stage starts, you pick a Blessing, buy a renovation, change a setting, or leave the app. Deleting the app deletes this saved progress.

Can I turn down effects or sound?

Open the menu (☰ in a stage, or Settings on the title screen) to adjust sound, haptics, screen shake, impact freeze, screen flashes, particles, game speed, swap controls, hints, score numbers and background motion.

How do I see what a Blessing does?

Press and hold any Blessing icon in the bottom-left corner during a stage.

The end-of-stage Spirit Rush is taking a while

Tap anywhere during the Spirit Rush to speed it up.

How do I start over?

Choose New Run on the title screen. Renovations bought at the Spirit Inn are permanent and carry over to new runs.
