Lantern Tide
A spirit inn puzzle

Match, chain, and light the way.

Lantern Tide is a match-3 roguelite set in a dusk-lit spirit world. Chain combos that kick the whole board, unleash Line Spirits, Lantern Bombs and Spirit Orbs, and earn Blessings that bend the rules of every run.

Travel through four worlds (Dusk Inn, Rain Town, Sea Rail and Steam Hall), then spend Inn Tokens on permanent renovations between runs.

Fully offline. No ads. No purchases. No data collected.
