Privacy Policy

Effective November 12, 2026

Lantern Tide does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information.

Information we collect

None. The app has no accounts or sign-in, and contains no advertising, analytics, tracking, or third-party SDKs. It does not use the network: the game runs entirely on your device.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress and settings (for example your current stage, level, Blessings, Inn Tokens and sound preferences) are stored only on your device, in the app's own storage. This data never leaves your device and is not sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app removes it.

Children's privacy

Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
